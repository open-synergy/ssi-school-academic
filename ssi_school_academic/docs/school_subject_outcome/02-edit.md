# Edit Subject Outcome

> **Module:** `ssi_school_academic`\
> **Model:** `school_subject_outcome`\
> **Menu:** School > Configuration > Subject & Scoring > Subject Outcomes\
> **Actor:** user in group `Subject Outcome`\
> **Requires:** `01-create`

## Pre-Condition

- **Record:** The Subject Outcome already exists.
- **Access:** User is in group `Subject Outcome`.

## Flow

1. Open the **School > Configuration > Subject & Scoring > Subject Outcomes** menu.
2. Find and open the record to edit, then click **Edit**.
3. Change the **Name**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Post-Condition

- The record is updated with the new name.
- The learning objectives already chosen on score sheets are kept.
